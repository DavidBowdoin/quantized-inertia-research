# Glossary

Terms used across this repository, in alphabetical order. Formulas are in [equations.md](equations.md).

**a₀ (MOND acceleration constant).** The empirical acceleration scale of MOND, ≈ 1.2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻², below which galaxy dynamics depart from Newtonian behaviour. In MOND it is fitted to data. QI claims to replace it with 2c²/Θ.

**Asymmetric Casimir effect.** McCulloch's 2013 mechanism for inertia. An accelerating object has a nearby Rindler horizon behind it and a distant cosmic horizon in front. These cut the Unruh spectrum differently on the two sides, which produces a net radiation pressure opposing the acceleration ([2013](../01-papers/2013-mcculloch-asymmetric-casimir/README.md)). In the propulsion papers the same idea is applied to cavities and metamaterials.

**Baryonic Tully–Fisher relation (BTFR).** The observed relation v⁴ ∝ M_baryonic for disc galaxies. QI gives v⁴ = 2GMc²/Θ ([2012](../01-papers/2012-mcculloch-galactic-scales/README.md)).

**β (Wien constant).** β = 1/x₅ ≈ 0.2014, where x₅ ≈ 4.965 solves 5(1 − e⁻ˣ) = x. It appears in the Unruh peak wavelength λₘ = 4π²βc²/a. QI papers round it to 0.2, and βπ² to 2.

**Casimir effect.** A measured attraction between closely spaced conducting plates. It arises because only vacuum modes that fit between the plates are allowed. QI borrows the idea at Hubble and Rindler scales. That borrowing is an assumption, not an established effect.

**Cosmic (Hubble) horizon.** In QI, the boundary of the observable universe, which bounds the Unruh waves an object can "see". It is placed at Θ/2 from the object.

**Co-moving diameter.** The present proper diameter of the observable universe, ≈ 8.8 × 10²⁶ m (≈ 93 billion light-years). It has been used as Θ since 2017 ([SPARC](../01-papers/2017-mcculloch-galaxy-rotations-sparc/README.md)).

**DARPA NLM.** DARPA's *Nascent Light-Matter Interactions* programme. In 2018 it funded about $1.3M over four years for QI theory (Plymouth) and experiments (TU Dresden; University of Alcalá) ([2018](../07-media/2018-plymouth-darpa/README.md)).

**Dark matter.** Unseen mass invoked in standard cosmology to explain galaxy and cluster dynamics, gravitational lensing and the CMB. QI and MOND both aim to remove the need for it in galaxies.

**EmDrive.** A tapered microwave cavity once claimed to produce thrust without propellant. McCulloch offered a QI explanation (2015, 2017). Precise tests at TU Dresden attributed the signals to artefacts ([2021](../03-criticism/2021-tajmar-emdrive-false-positive/README.md)).

**Equivalence principle (weak).** The principle that inertial and gravitational mass are equal, so all bodies fall alike. QI breaks it at low accelerations, by amounts McCulloch argues are too small for torsion-balance or free-fall tests to detect.

**EUP (Extended Uncertainty Principle).** A generalisation of Heisenberg's principle with a large-scale correction term. Giné & Luciano used it to derive QI-like modified inertia ([2020](../01-papers/2020-gine-luciano-eup-modified-inertia/README.md)).

**External field effect (EFE).** In MOND, the internal dynamics of a system depend on the external gravitational field it sits in. This matters for wide-binary tests.

**Flyby anomaly.** Small unexplained velocity changes reported in some Earth gravity-assist flybys (Galileo, NEAR, Rosetta and others). QI modelled them in 2008. Whether the anomaly is real is itself unsettled.

**Haisch–Rueda–Puthoff (HRP) model.** A 1994 proposal that inertia comes from a Lorentz force produced by the zero-point field. It was an inspiration for QI. QI papers criticise it for needing an arbitrary high-frequency cutoff.

**Heisenberg uncertainty principle (as used in QI).** ΔpΔx ≳ ħ/2. In the 2016 and 2020 derivations the horizons limit Δx, which gives an energy ħc/Δx that changes with acceleration.

**Hubble constant (H, H₀).** The present expansion rate of the universe. QI's 2007 paper uses H = 2.3 × 10⁻¹⁸ s⁻¹ (≈ 71 km s⁻¹ Mpc⁻¹).

**Hubble diameter (Θ).** QI's cosmic length scale. It was defined in 2007 as Θ = 2c/H ≈ 2.6 × 10²⁶ m, though the papers print 2.7 × 10²⁶ m, and has been taken as 8.8 × 10²⁶ m since 2017. See [equations.md §7](equations.md#7-discrepancies-and-inconsistencies-between-papers).

**Hubble-scale Casimir effect.** QI's central assumption. Only Unruh wavelengths λₙ = 2Θ/n are allowed, so long waves are increasingly "disallowed" at low acceleration and inertia falls ([2007](../01-papers/2007-mcculloch-pioneer-anomaly/README.md)).

**Inertial mass (m_i, m_I).** The resistance of a body to acceleration (F = m_i a). It is distinct in principle from gravitational mass, which sets the gravitational force on a body.

**IVO Quantum Drive.** A capacitor-based, propellant-free thruster made by IVO Ltd. and claimed to work by QI. Its first flight (Barry-1, 2023–24) failed before the drive was tested. McCulloch called its second (OTP-2, 2025) inconclusive ([2026](../07-media/2026-nextbigfuture-ivo-review/README.md)).

**JuMBOs.** Jupiter-mass binary objects, observed in the Trapezium cluster (Orion). McCulloch (2025) says their mutual accelerations level off near 2c²/Θ ([2025](../01-papers/2025-mcculloch-jupiter-mass-binaries/README.md)).

**Landauer principle.** Erasing one bit of information costs at least k_BT ln 2 of energy. It was used in McCulloch's 2020 information-theory derivation.

**Mach's principle.** The idea that inertia arises from a body's relation to all the other matter in the universe. QI describes the |a| in its formula as acceleration relative to surrounding matter ([2010](../01-papers/2010-mcculloch-minimum-accelerations/README.md)).

**MiHsC.** *Modification of inertia resulting from a Hubble-scale Casimir effect*, the original name of QI (2007).

**Minimum acceleration.** QI's prediction that no object's acceleration can settle below about 2c²/Θ (≈ 6.9 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻² with the old Θ, ≈ 2.0 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻² with the new one) ([2010](../01-papers/2010-mcculloch-minimum-accelerations/README.md)).

**MOND.** Modified Newtonian Dynamics (Milgrom 1983). An empirical modification of gravity or inertia below a₀ that fits galaxy rotation curves. It can be read either as modified gravity or as modified inertia.

**Pioneer anomaly.** A small, roughly constant sunward acceleration of ~8.7 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻² seen in tracking data from Pioneer 10 and 11. It was QI's first target (2007) and was explained in 2012 as anisotropic thermal recoil ([Turyshev et al.](../03-criticism/2012-turyshev-pioneer-thermal/README.md)).

**Planck length / mass (l_P, m_P).** 1.616 × 10⁻³⁵ m and 2.176 × 10⁻⁸ kg. They are used in the 2013 and 2016 particle-mass estimates.

**Podkletnov effect.** A claimed weight reduction of objects above rotating superconductors. It has not been confirmed. QI offered an explanation in 2011.

**Quantized / Quantised Inertia (QI).** The later name for MiHsC. "Quantised" refers to the discrete set of allowed Unruh wavelengths, not to standard quantisation of a field.

**Radial acceleration relation (RAR).** The tight empirical relation between observed acceleration and baryonic (Newtonian) acceleration in galaxies (McGaugh et al. 2016, from SPARC). QI predicts a = a_b√(1 + 2c²/(a_bΘ)).

**Rindler horizon.** For an observer with constant proper acceleration a, the boundary at distance ≈ c²/a behind them, beyond which signals can never reach them.

**SPARC.** *Spitzer Photometry and Accurate Rotation Curves*, a database of 175 disc galaxies. The RAR study used 153 of them.

**SpaceDrive.** The TU Dresden programme (Tajmar group) for testing propellantless-propulsion claims, including EmDrive, Mach-effect and QI set-ups ([2018](../04-experiments/2018-tajmar-spacedrive/README.md)).

**Tajmar effect.** Anomalous gyroscope accelerations reported near spinning, cryogenically cooled rings (Tajmar et al., around 2006–2008). QI modelled it in 2010–11. It has not been established.

> [!UNVERIFIED]
> Whether later work by Tajmar's own group or others failed to reproduce the Tajmar effect is not documented in the sources collected so far.

**Unruh effect / Unruh radiation.** The prediction of quantum field theory (Fulling 1973, Davies 1975, Unruh 1976) that a uniformly accelerating observer sees the vacuum as a thermal bath at T = ħa/(2πck_B). It has not been directly observed. Its possible anisotropy is studied by Kholupenko et al. ([Part I](../01-papers/2019-kholupenko-unruh-anisotropy-1/README.md)).

**Wide binaries.** Pairs of stars separated by thousands of AU, whose mutual accelerations fall below ~10⁻⁹ m s⁻². They are a contested test ground for MOND and QI ([Banik et al.](../03-criticism/2023-banik-gaia-dr3-wide-binaries/README.md); [Chae](../01-papers/2023-chae-wide-binaries/README.md); [McCulloch & Lucio](../01-papers/2019-mcculloch-wide-binaries/README.md)).
