# Holographic principle

## Plain-language explanation

Black-hole thermodynamics (Bekenstein 1973; Hawking 1975) says a black hole's entropy is proportional to the **area** of its horizon, not its volume. 't Hooft (1993) and Susskind (1995) generalised this into the **holographic principle**: the maximum amount of information that can be stored in a region of space scales with its bounding area, at about one bit per few Planck areas. The idea suggests that a theory of quantum gravity in a volume can be described by degrees of freedom living on its boundary. Its best-understood realisation is the **AdS/CFT correspondence** (Maldacena 1997), in which a gravitational theory in anti-de Sitter space is exactly equivalent to a non-gravitational quantum field theory on its boundary.

Related programmes treat gravity itself as emergent and thermodynamic: Jacobson (1995) derived Einstein's equations from horizon thermodynamics, and **Verlinde (2011)** proposed "entropic gravity", deriving Newton's law from information on holographic screens plus the Unruh temperature. Verlinde (2017) extended this to argue that de Sitter entropy produces an extra MOND-like force in galaxies.

## Key equations

Bekenstein–Hawking entropy / holographic bound:

$$
S \le \frac{k_B A}{4\ell_P^2}, \qquad \ell_P^2 = \frac{G\hbar}{c^3}
$$

Verlinde's entropic force (holographic screen at distance $\Delta x$, Unruh temperature):

$$
F\,\Delta x = T\,\Delta S,\qquad \Delta S = 2\pi k_B\frac{mc}{\hbar}\Delta x,\qquad k_B T = \frac{\hbar a}{2\pi c}\ \Rightarrow\ F = ma
$$

## Mainstream status

- Black-hole entropy and the holographic bound are widely accepted theoretical results. AdS/CFT is one of the most studied ideas in theoretical physics, but our universe is not AdS, and holography for de Sitter / cosmological spacetimes is not established.
- Entropic gravity (Verlinde) is speculative and contested. Brouwer et al. (2017) found galaxy–galaxy lensing consistent with Verlinde's 2017 prediction for isolated galaxies, but Lelli, McGaugh & Schombert (2017) found it inconsistent with the radial acceleration relation at large radii, and other tests have been mixed.
- None of these frameworks is experimentally confirmed.

## How it relates to QI

Later QI derivations recast inertia in information/holographic language:

- McCulloch & Giné (2017) derive QI from information lost behind Rindler and Hubble horizons ([source](../01-papers/2017-mcculloch-information-loss/README.md)); McCulloch (2020) from Landauer's principle ([source](../01-papers/2020-mcculloch-information-theory/README.md)).
- Giné connects Verlinde's holographic gravity with MiHsC, the Pioneer anomaly and cosmology ([2011](../01-papers/2011-gine-pioneer-holographic/README.md), [2012 MPLA](../01-papers/2012-gine-holographic-modified-inertia/README.md), [2012 cosmology](../01-papers/2012-gine-cosmological-holographic/README.md), [2017 particle masses](../01-papers/2017-gine-inertial-mass-holographic/README.md)).
- Smolin (2017) and Cadoni & Tuveri (2019) propose horizon/quantum-gravity origins for MOND-like behaviour and cite MiHsC as related ([Smolin](../01-papers/2017-smolin-mond-quantum-gravity/README.md), [Cadoni](../01-papers/2019-cadoni-long-range-qg/README.md)).

These links show QI sits within a broader, legitimate (if speculative) research theme: **horizons, information and inertia/gravity at low accelerations**. The QI-specific claims (the precise formula, propellantless thrust) are not implied by holography; they are additional hypotheses.
