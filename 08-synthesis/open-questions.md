# Open questions

> **Editorial.** These are questions the literature has not settled, as far as we could find. A question being here does not mean it is unanswerable, only that no published answer has been found yet.

## Theory
1. **Which Θ is correct, and why?** Is it the Hubble diameter 2c/H (≈ 2.6 × 10²⁶ m) or the comoving diameter (≈ 8.8 × 10²⁶ m)? Is there a derivation that fixes Θ *before* comparing with galaxy data? ([equations §7](../00-overview/equations.md#7-discrepancies-and-inconsistencies-between-papers))
2. **Does any version of the derivation survive Renda's and Dickmann's critiques?** McCulloch's 2026 blog response says the formula can be re-derived from the uncertainty principle and from information erasure at horizons. Neither route has had independent review ([derivation](../02-evidence/derivation.md), [Renda 2019](../03-criticism/2019-renda-sceptical/README.md), [Dickmann 2023](../03-criticism/2023-dickmann-casimir-contradicts/README.md)).
3. **Momentum and energy.** Where does the momentum go in a QI thruster? How does a thrust-to-power ratio about 10⁷ times the photon limit avoid over-unity energy at high speed? ([conservation-of-momentum](../05-context/conservation-of-momentum.md))
4. **Equivalence principle.** QI modifies inertial mass but not gravitational mass. What does that predict for torsion-balance / MICROSCOPE-class tests at low accelerations? ([equivalence-principle](../05-context/equivalence-principle.md))
5. **Is Unruh radiation real and directional enough?** QI needs an anisotropic Unruh field. Kholupenko's papers examine anisotropy, and Lynch et al. look for an Unruh-like thermometer signal. Neither has tested QI directly ([unruh-effect](../05-context/unruh-effect.md)).
6. **No relativistic or field-theory formulation.** There is no QI action, no field equations and no covariant formulation, so lensing and cosmology cannot be computed.

## Astrophysics
7. **Bullet Cluster, CMB acoustic peaks, structure formation.** We found no QI calculation of any of these ([dark-matter](../05-context/dark-matter.md)).
8. **External field effect.** QI claims no external field effect, but the groups that see a wide-binary anomaly fit MOND *with* one. Can the external field effect be measured cleanly enough to separate QI from MOND? ([wide-binaries](../02-evidence/wide-binaries.md))
9. **Redshift dependence.** QI predicts a₀ grows with (1+z) as horizons shrink. High-z rotation curves (Genzel/Übler) are a test. Has anyone fitted them quantitatively with QI? ([galaxy-rotation](../02-evidence/galaxy-rotation.md))
10. **Globular clusters**: there is no QI prediction in print ([globular-clusters](../02-evidence/globular-clusters.md)).

## Experiment
11. **IVO OTP-2 data.** The only account of it is McCulloch's own report, relayed by NextBigFuture (2026). Will orbit-decay data be published for independent analysis? ([2026 NextBigFuture](../07-media/2026-nextbigfuture-ivo-review/README.md), [ivo-ltd](../06-people-orgs/ivo-ltd.md))
12. **DARPA 2018 programme.** No final report is public; the Alcalá results are not published in peer-reviewed form ([darpa-2018-award](../06-people-orgs/darpa-2018-award.md)).
13. **Bhatt & Becker capacitor result.** It has not been replicated outside Plymouth, and Tajmar & Schreiber's test found < 0.3 mg where 6.8 mg was predicted ([2018 Bhatt](../04-experiments/2018-bhatt-capacitor-thrust/README.md), [2020 Tajmar](../03-criticism/2020-tajmar-capacitor-thrust/README.md)).
