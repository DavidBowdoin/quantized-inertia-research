# Quantised Accelerations: From Anomalies to New Physics

**Citation:** McCulloch, M. E. (2024). *Quantised Accelerations: From Anomalies to New Physics*. Polaris Books. ISBN 979-8-9902823-1-5 (paperback). Retailer listing gives release date 15 July 2024 and 224 pages; one reviewer gives 216 pages.

**TL;DR:** McCulloch's second book. Per the publisher's description it surveys 54 anomalies "where nature falsifies physics" and argues they motivate quantised inertia (QI), in which accelerations arise from horizon-induced gradients in quantum fields, removing the need for dark matter and offering a route to propellant-less thrust.

## Key claims
- From the publisher blurb (as quoted in retailer listings and search snippets): the book explores 54 anomalies; based on them the author proposes QI; all accelerations arise from horizon-induced gradients in quantum fields; QI removes the need for dark matter (the blurb reportedly also says for Newton's gravitational constant); and it suggests propellant-less thrust.
- These are the author's claims, not independently assessed.
