# Quantized Inertia is Nonsense

**Citation:** Fleming, R. (2022, 25 February, premiere). *Quantized Inertia is Nonsense* [video]. YouTube. <https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=dM2sBIza_Yg> (accessed 2026-10-19). The title and channel ("Ray Fleming", @rayfleming2053) were confirmed via YouTube oEmbed. The premiere date and description come from the watch page.

**TL;DR:** A YouTube video by Ray Fleming, an independent author who promotes his own vacuum-fluctuation ("zero-point" / "onium") models of physics. He argues that QI's mechanism is physically unfounded. The video description says that an accelerating observer does not affect quantum fluctuations unless they are hit; that Unruh radiation "requires two separate realities which can't happen"; and that neither the Rindler horizon nor the cosmic horizon is a physical boundary that could produce a Casimir effect. **Only the video description has been read for this entry; the video itself was not transcribed.**

## What the article says
Based on the video description only:
- QI (MiHsC) is "a controversial theory that attempts to explain the dark matter problem and the EMDrive". It rests on an accelerating observer experiencing Unruh radiation, which produces a force differential "loosely based on the Casimir effect".
- "an accelerating observer does not affect quantum fluctuations unless they are hit".
- "Unruh radiation requires two separate realities which can't happen."
- "the Rindler horizon and the cosmic horizon are not physical boundaries that could cause a Casimir effect".
- The rest of the description lists Fleming's own ResearchGate papers (onium particle models, "General relativity as a quantum van der Waals torque effect", and others) and his books (*The Zero-Point Universe*, *The 100 Greatest Lies in Physics*, *Goodbye Quarks*).

## Claims & caveats
- The third point, that a horizon is not a physical boundary, matches the core argument of the more formal [Junghanns 2026](../../03-criticism/2026-junghanns-causal-restriction-nogo/README.md) no-go paper. The first point, about Unruh radiation and inertia, overlaps with [Koberlein 2017](../2017-koberlein-doing-it-wrong/README.md). The second point, that Unruh radiation "requires two separate realities", rejects the Unruh effect itself, which is a standard QFT result (see [unruh-effect](../../05-context/unruh-effect.md)). That puts Fleming at odds with mainstream physics as well as with QI.
