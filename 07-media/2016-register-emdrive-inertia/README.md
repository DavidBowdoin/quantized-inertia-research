# 'Impossible' EmDrive flying saucer thruster may herald new theory of inertia

**Citation:** Andrew Orlowski (2016-04-21). *'Impossible' EmDrive flying saucer thruster may herald new theory of inertia*. The Register. <https://www.theregister.com/2016/04/21/em_drive_theory_why/> (Accessed 2026-10-18.)

**TL;DR:** Short Register piece pointing readers to McCulloch's arXiv paper (1604.03449) as "a new, and testable, theory of inertia" that could explain EmDrive thrust and the flyby anomaly.

## What the article says
- Describes the EmDrive (proposed by Roger Shawyer in 1999) and the mainstream objection that it would violate conservation of momentum.
- Notes reported thrust from tests in Germany, China and at NASA is tiny and "within the margin of error".
- Summarises McCulloch's idea: radiation pressure from Unruh waves changes momentum as the thruster moves; the same effect is offered for spacecraft flyby jumps. Links the arXiv paper and McCulloch's blog.

## Who is quoted
- Nobody is quoted directly; McCulloch's views are paraphrased. The author's own phrase "the scientific priesthood" refers to mainstream scientists.

## Claims & caveats
- No experimental confirmation of McCulloch's predictions is reported; the theory is presented as something to be tested.

## Tone
- Playful ("Boffin", "flying saucer"), with scare quotes on "Impossible"; neither endorses nor rebuts.
