# EXCLUSIVE: The 'Impossible' Quantum Drive That Defies Known Laws of Physics Was Just Launched Into Space

**Citation:** Christopher Plain (2023-11-14). *EXCLUSIVE: The 'Impossible' Quantum Drive That Defies Known Laws of Physics Was Just Launched Into Space*. The Debrief. <https://thedebrief.org/exclusive-the-impossible-quantum-drive-that-defies-known-laws-of-physics-was-just-launched-into-space/> (Accessed 2026-10-18.)

**TL;DR:** Reports that IVO's Quantum Drives launched on 11 Nov 2023 aboard Rogue Space Systems' Barry-1 on SpaceX Transporter-9, with a plan for weeks of no-thrust baseline data before attempting to raise, lower and then change the satellite's orbit.

## What the article says
- Launch Saturday 11 Nov 2023 on Transporter-9 (80+ payloads to LEO); Rogue made contact on 13 Nov and began the launch and early orbit phase (LEOP).
- Plan: several weeks of baseline data without thrust, then raise the orbit, lower it, and test inclination changes.
- IVO claims 100+ hours of prototype testing in simulated space conditions and a 1,000-hour stress test, with lab tests giving the "predicted" thrust (no numbers given).
- An earlier June 2023 flight was cancelled due to a glitch elsewhere on the satellite.
- The drive is based on McCulloch's QI, which the article says has drawn wide criticism; nearly all mainstream scientists say it should not work.

## Who is quoted
- **Richard Mansell** (IVO founder): "Launch and deployment were successful!"; "Our goal is to raise the orbit".
- **Mike McCulloch** (quoted from his website) on Newton's first law.

## Claims & caveats
- Thrust performance claims are the company's; no independent data cited.

## Tone
- Enthusiastic and sensational headline; body largely relays company statements with some sceptical context.

## Criticisms & responses
- No independent, peer-reviewed measurement of IVO Quantum Drive thrust has been published (as of the sources reviewed). Lab tests of related capacitor-based QI thrust claims at TU Dresden found no thrust beyond artefacts: [2020-tajmar-capacitor-thrust](../../03-criticism/2020-tajmar-capacitor-thrust/README.md).
- Andrew Higgins (McGill) argues any propellantless electromagnetic drive exceeding about 3.33 µN/kW could be turned into a perpetual-motion machine (quoted in [2023-universetoday-ivo](../2023-universetoday-ivo/README.md)).
