# DARPA invests in propellant-free rocket theory

**Citation:** Brooks Hays (2018-09-26). *DARPA invests in propellant-free rocket theory*. UPI. <https://www.upi.com/DARPA-invests-in-propellant-free-rocket-theory/7121537904594/> (Accessed 2026-10-18.)

**TL;DR:** UPI news report on the $1.3M DARPA grant, notable for quoting DARPA programme manager Mike Fiddy on why the agency funds such testable ideas.

## What the article says
- Grant first funds a postdoc and a numerical model; after about 18 months, two thrust experiments in Germany and Spain.
- McCulloch also claims QI explains galaxy rotation without dark matter and EmDrive thrust; the article notes an earlier study questioned whether the EmDrive produces thrust at all.

## Who is quoted
- **Mike McCulloch**: "I believe that the EmDrive is a manifestation of quantized inertia."; "I have gotten a lot of resistance from people who believe in dark matter."
- **Mike Fiddy** (DARPA NLM programme manager): "When these new ideas are relevant to DARPA's mission and are testable, the agency may well support them."

## Claims & caveats
- Theory not yet fully predictive (model still to be built); experiments planned, not built.

## Tone
- Mostly neutral news; light humour and some scepticism.

## Notes
- Article contains the typo "Uhler radiation" for Unruh radiation.

## Criticisms & responses
- Laser-resonator and capacitor experiments at TU Dresden (the experimental partner named in the DARPA project) reported no thrust attributable to QI: [2022-neunzig-laser-resonators](../../03-criticism/2022-neunzig-laser-resonators/README.md); see also [2020-tajmar-capacitor-thrust](../../03-criticism/2020-tajmar-capacitor-thrust/README.md).
