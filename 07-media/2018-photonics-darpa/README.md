# Plymouth Researchers Awarded $1.3M Grant for Light-Based Space Travel

**Citation:** Photonics Media (2018-10-26). *Plymouth Researchers Awarded $1.3M Grant for Light-Based Space Travel*. Photonics.com / Photonics Spectra. <https://www.photonics.com/Articles/Plymouth_Researchers_Awarded_13M_Grant_for/a64062> (Accessed 2026-10-18.)

**TL;DR:** Trade-press rewrite of the University of Plymouth release on the $1.3M DARPA NLM grant, with McCulloch's quotes about converting light into thrust.

## What the article says
- Restates the release: QI predicts objects can be pushed by differences in Unruh-radiation intensity (ship-and-dock wave analogy); a shield/damper above an accelerated system (spinning disc or light between mirrors) should produce thrust.
- Funding via DARPA's Nascent Light-Matter Interactions (NLM) programme; collaborators at TU Dresden and University of Alcalá; first 18 months theory, then experiments in Germany and Spain.

## Who is quoted
- **Mike McCulloch**: "I believe QI could be a real game-changer for space science."; "It has the potential to make interplanetary travel much easier and interstellar travel possible."

- No independent comment or caveats.
