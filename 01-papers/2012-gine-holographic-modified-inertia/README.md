# The holographic scenario, the modified inertia and the dynamics of the universe

**Citation:** Giné, J. (2012). *The holographic scenario, the modified inertia and the dynamics of the universe*. Modern Physics Letters A 27(34), 1250208. [doi:10.1142/S0217732312502082](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0217732312502082)

**TL;DR:** According to its abstract, this paper "attempts to connect" Verlinde's holographic gravity with McCulloch's MiHsC. It uses two holographic screens: one at roughly the Hubble horizon, and one that accounts for the matter between the test particle and the observer. Together these give a modified Friedmann acceleration equation. With quantum corrections, the paper says this equation gives both inflation and late-time acceleration without dark energy. A MOND-like law follows from both models. A "first connection" between the two theories is given at the end.

> PDF not retrievable: paywalled (World Scientific), and no arXiv version was found. The summary below is based only on the abstract (from Crossref/OpenAlex) and the Crossref reference list.

## Key claims (from the abstract)
- The holographic scenario "can give the correct dynamics of the universe".
- Two holographic screens give "directly the modified Friedmann acceleration equation for the dynamical evolution of the universe":
  - one screen comparable to the Hubble horizon;
  - one that takes into account the matter between the test particle and the observer.
- With quantum corrections, the equation "will realize the inflation at high energy scales and the late-time acceleration … obviating the dark energy".
- "From both models we can derive a version of Modified Newtonian Dynamics (MOND) … obviating the dark matter and explaining other astronomical anomalies."
- "A first connection between both theories is given at the end of the paper."
