# Thrust from Symmetric Capacitors using Quantised Inertia

**Citation:** McCulloch, M. E. (2021). *Thrust from Symmetric Capacitors using Quantised Inertia*. ResearchGate preprint 353481953 (not peer-reviewed). https://www.researchgate.net/publication/353481953_Thrust_from_Symmetric_Capacitors_using_Quantised_Inertia

> [!UNVERIFIED] PDF not retrievable. ResearchGate blocks automated access (HTTP 403), and no arXiv, journal or institutional copy was found. The contents below come only from McCulloch's own blog descriptions, *Physics from the Edge*, 2021–2026. The preprint text itself has not been checked.

Per the batch notes, the blog says the preprint was submitted to a journal (JPC), and a "Version 2" exists on ResearchGate. No published version was found as of 2026-10.

**TL;DR (from the author's blog):** The preprint applies QI to the thrust reported by Becker & Bhatt (2018) from symmetric capacitors under field-emission conditions. In McCulloch's picture, electrons tunnelling across a thin dielectric at very high acceleration see Unruh radiation everywhere except between the plates, a Casimir-like "quantum void". This pulls electrons out of the cathode faster than expected and gives a net push towards the anode. The blog gives the resulting prediction as F = 0.00014 · IA/d².

## Key claims
As described on the author's blog. These have not been verified against the preprint.
- The preprint's predictions are described as matching "the results of Becker and Bhatt and Mansell exactly, even the changes as you vary the plate separation". Source: blog post "A Thrust from 'Nothing'", 2021-11.
- The thrust is directed towards the anode.
- In a 2026 post, McCulloch describes the 2021 derivation as using "dimensional and physical arguments". He says it predicts thrust measurements from labs in California (Becker & Bhatt), Virginia (IVO Ltd), Italy and Plymouth.

## Key equations
Blog-reported thrust formula. Here I is the (Fowler–Nordheim) leakage current, A the plate area and d the plate separation; units are not stated on the blog.
$$ F = 0.00014\,\frac{IA}{d^2} $$
> [!UNVERIFIED] It has not been checked whether this exact form appears in the preprint. It is cited to "McCulloch (2021)" in the blog posts "How to (Maybe) See QI in Your Lab" (2022-07) and "Glimpses of QI in the Lab?" (2022-08).

## Data used
- Thrust data from Becker & Bhatt (2018) and from IVO Ltd (R. Mansell), as described on the blog.

## Results & predictions
- The blog ("On the Cusp?", 2022-11) reports Plymouth lab tests in which an extracted anode-directed force of "about 10 milligrams" was "only 1/3 of the force predicted (McCulloch, 2021)".
- The blog ("How to (Maybe) See QI in Your Lab", 2022-07) says: "In our 22 tests so far 14% of them have shown a thrust."

## Criticisms & responses
- **Independent capacitor test:** [Tajmar et al. 2020 (2020-tajmar-capacitor-thrust)](../../03-criticism/2020-tajmar-capacitor-thrust/README.md) tested charged high-voltage capacitors in vacuum. They reported strong limits on electrostatic-propulsion claims.
  - > [!UNVERIFIED] Whether that study tested the field-emission regime of Becker & Bhatt specifically is covered in its own folder, not here.
- **Becker & Bhatt result:** the underlying Becker & Bhatt measurement ([2018-bhatt-capacitor-thrust](../../04-experiments/2018-bhatt-capacitor-thrust/README.md), arXiv:[1810.04368](https://arxiv.org/abs/1810.04368)) has not been independently replicated in the peer-reviewed literature, according to this repository's notes on that source.
- **Spaceflight test:** IVO Ltd's in-orbit "QI thruster" demonstration on Barry-1 (2023–24) ended after the satellite suffered power-system problems and lost contact. No thrust result was published. See [2024-debrief-ivo-failure](../../07-media/2024-debrief-ivo-failure/README.md).
- General QI critique: [Renda 2019](../../03-criticism/2019-renda-sceptical/README.md).
