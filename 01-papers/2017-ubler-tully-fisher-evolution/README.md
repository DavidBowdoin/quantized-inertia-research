# The Evolution of the Tully–Fisher Relation between z ∼ 2.3 and z ∼ 0.9 with KMOS3D

**Citation:** Übler, H., Förster Schreiber, N. M., Genzel, R., et al. (2017). *The Evolution of the Tully–Fisher Relation between z ∼ 2.3 and z ∼ 0.9 with KMOS3D*. The Astrophysical Journal 842, 121. https://doi.org/10.3847/1538-4357/aa7558. Preprint: [arXiv:1703.04321](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04321) (`paper.pdf` is arXiv v2).

**TL;DR:** Stellar and baryonic Tully–Fisher relations for KMOS3D discs at z ≈ 0.9 and 2.3, corrected for pressure support. Both relations lie below the local ones at z ≈ 0.9. Between z ≈ 0.9 and 2.3 the stellar relation does not evolve, while the baryonic one rises. The authors explain the non-monotonic evolution with changing gas and dark-matter fractions. McCulloch 2017 cited this paper in support of QI.

## Key claims
- At fixed circular velocity, baryonic masses are higher at z ≈ 2.3 than at z ≈ 0.9, and stellar masses are similar (Abstract).
- With local slopes, the baryonic zero point is b = 10.68 ± 0.04 at z ≈ 0.9 and 10.85 ± 0.05 at z ≈ 2.3, a change of +0.17 dex (Sect. 3.3).
- Against Lelli et al. 2016, the baryonic offsets are −0.44 and −0.27 dex; against Reyes et al. 2011, the stellar offsets are −0.44 and −0.42 dex (Fig. 5).

## Key equations
Fixed-slope fits of log M = a log(v_c/v_ref) + b, with v_ref = 242 km s⁻¹ and local slopes a = 3.60 (stellar) and 3.75 (baryonic) (Sect. 3.3).

## Data used
- 135 rotation-dominated KMOS3D discs at 0.6 < z < 2.6 with modelled circular velocities, split into z ≈ 0.9 and z ≈ 2.3 subsamples (Fig. 4, Sect. 3.2).
- Baryonic masses add gas masses from scaling relations to SED stellar masses.

## Results & predictions
- A toy model with redshift-dependent gas fractions and baryon-to-dark-matter ratios reproduces the non-monotonic evolution (Sect. 4).

## Criticisms & responses
- Editorial: McCulloch 2017 quotes observed reductions of −0.38 to −0.47 dex (z = 0.9) and −0.2 to −0.47 dex (z = 2.3). QI's Eq. 13 predicts a further fall of 0.24 dex between those redshifts, where this paper finds a rise of 0.17 ± 0.06 dex ([A6d](../../09-analysis/a6d-mcculloch-2017-highz/README.md)).
