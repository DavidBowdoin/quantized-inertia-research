# Measuring Planck scale U(1) gauge interactions in Quantised Inertia using IKKT gravity

**Citation:** Soli, G. (2021). *Measuring Planck scale U(1) gauge interactions in Quantised Inertia using IKKT gravity*. ResearchGate preprint, https://doi.org/10.13140/RG.2.2.31904.81929/2. Also presented as a poster abstract at the APS April Meeting 2021: https://meetings-archive.aps.org/apr/2021/kp01/108

> [!UNVERIFIED] PDF not retrievable. ResearchGate, the APS meeting archive and the APS abstract image (absimage.aps.org) all returned HTTP 403 to automated requests. The description below comes from a web-search summary of the APS abstract page and has not been checked against the full abstract text or the preprint.

**TL;DR:** A single-author poster abstract and preprint. The APS listing gives the author's affiliation as "retired". It proposes a theoretical foundation for measuring quantum-statistical effects on inertia and gravity. Inertia is treated as an entropic force emerging from the IKKT (type IIB) matrix model through "mild" UV/IR mixing, with the dark-energy density named as the source of the fluctuations. The proposed experiment would use optical pressure in fibre-optic loops to produce spontaneously emitted Unruh photons and superradiant photon pairs.

## Key claims
As summarised from the APS abstract page; unverified in detail.
- Inertia is "an entropic force that is emergent from the IKKT matrix model". It is caused by "mild" UV/IR mixing.
- Fibre-optic loops under optical pressure could produce Unruh photons and superradiant photon pairs, allowing a test of quantum-statistical effects on inertia.
- The work frames these ideas as a route to measuring Planck-scale U(1) gauge interactions within QI.
