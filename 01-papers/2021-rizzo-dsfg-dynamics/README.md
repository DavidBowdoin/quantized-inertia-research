Preprint: [arXiv:2102.05671](https://arxiv.org/abs/2102.05671) (`paper.pdf` is the arXiv version).

**TL;DR:** ALMA [CII] kinematics of five lensed dusty star-forming galaxies at z ≈ 4.2–4.8, with sub-kpc resolution. All are rotation-dominated discs with V/σ ≈ 7–15. Rotation-curve decompositions into a Sérsic stellar component, exponential gas disc and NFW halo find bulge signatures in most. The paper does not mention QI; its Tables 4, 5 and C1 are the inputs to our [A6f](../../09-analysis/a6f-rizzo-z45-qi/README.md).

## Key claims
- All galaxies in the combined sample are dynamically cold, with V/σ between 7 and 15 (Abstract).
- Stellar feedback is sufficient to sustain the measured turbulence (Abstract).
- Five of six galaxies show the dynamical signature of a bulge (Abstract).
- The baryon-to-total mass ratio at the outermost radius ranges from 0.66 ± 0.02 (SPT0441-46) to 0.90 ± 0.03 (SPT0345-47) (Sect. 4).

## Key equations
NFW halo velocity with fixed concentration (Eq. 10); deprojected Sérsic stellar profile following Lima Neto et al. 1999 and Terzić & Graham 2005 (Sect. 3.3).

## Data used
- ALMA [CII] observations of SPT0113-46, SPT0345-47, SPT0441-46, SPT2146-55 and SPT2132-58, with lens modelling (Tables 1–3).
- Priors: flat in R_e, n and α[CII]; log-uniform in stellar and halo mass (Table C2). Concentrations fixed from Dutton & Macciò 2014 (Table C1).
