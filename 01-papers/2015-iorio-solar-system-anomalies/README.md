# Gravitational anomalies in the solar system?

Read the paper at [arXiv:1412.7673](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.7673) · [doi:10.1142/S0218271815300153](https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218271815300153).

**Citation:** Iorio, L. (2015). *Gravitational anomalies in the solar system?* International Journal of Modern Physics D 24(6), 1530015 (invited review). https://doi.org/10.1142/S0218271815300153. Preprint: [arXiv:1412.7673](https://arxiv.org/abs/1412.7673) (`paper.pdf` is arXiv v4, 16 Mar 2015; 35 pp.).

**TL;DR:** This invited review critically surveys eight alleged solar-system gravitational anomalies:
- planetary perihelion precessions
- the orbit of Uranus's moon Mab
- the secular increase of the Moon's eccentricity
- the Faint Young Sun Paradox
- the secular decrease of the Sun's GM
- the flyby anomaly
- the Pioneer anomaly
- the secular increase of the astronomical unit

Iorio concludes that the Pioneer anomaly has a conventional thermal explanation, and that the AU "anomaly" disappeared when the AU was redefined in 2012. The flyby anomaly is statistically real but unexplained. For McCulloch's QI/MiHsC, the review matters mostly by context: McCulloch's 2008 MNRAS flyby paper is cited (ref. 211) only as one of several "modified" gravitational explanations, none of which "has proved itself fully satisfactorily so far".

## Key claims
- **Flyby anomaly (Sec. 7).** Unexplained Δv∞ ≈ 1–10 mm/s at Earth flybys of Galileo, NEAR, Cassini, Rosetta and possibly Juno, with uncertainties as small as 0.05–0.1 mm/s. It was absent at other flybys, such as Rosetta's 2nd and 3rd. The Anderson et al. empirical formula (Eq. 28) does not contain G and "failed to account for the null result" of the later Rosetta flybys. The review gives a general test for any non-standard explanation: it must be tested independently of the flyby anomaly, and must explain why the effect does not show up in bound Earth satellites.
- **Pioneer anomaly (Sec. 8).** Reported as A_Pio = (8.74 ± 1.33)×10⁻¹⁰ m/s² towards the Sun at 20–70 au. Since 2012, thermal recoil models explain ~80% of it, and the remaining 20% is not statistically significant (Turyshev et al. 2012). Independent thermal studies in 2012 and 2014 agree. Ephemerides of Uranus, Neptune, Pluto and TNOs also rule out a Pioneer-like sunward acceleration acting on natural bodies. The TNO fit gave A = (0.87 ± 1.6)×10⁻¹⁰ m/s², compatible with zero.
- **Astronomical unit (Sec. 9).** The reported increase of 0.15 ± 0.04 m/yr was tied to the old IAU 1976 Gaussian-constant definition. Since IAU 2012 Resolution B2, 1 au = 149,597,870,700 m exactly.
- **Perihelia (Sec. 2).** The Saturn anomaly faded with newer data. Non-zero EPM corrections for Venus and Jupiter are only 1.6–2 times their uncertainties.
- **Mab (Sec. 3).** Unexplained residuals of up to ~280 km. A conventional explanation via unseen moonlets has been proposed.
- **Lunar eccentricity (Sec. 4).** An unexplained secular rate of ~10⁻¹² yr⁻¹ remains.
- **General methodology (Sec. 10).** An anomaly must be confirmed by independent analyses, and conventional causes must be excluded. Any proposed gravitational mechanism must also be tested in other systems.

## Key equations
Anderson et al. empirical flyby formula (Eq. 28):
$$\frac{\Delta v_\infty}{v_\infty} = \frac{2\Omega_\oplus R_\oplus}{c}\left(\cos\delta_{in} - \cos\delta_{out}\right)$$

Pioneer frequency drift and inferred acceleration (Eqs. 29–30):
$$\Delta\dot\nu = (5.99 \pm 0.01)\times10^{-9}\ \text{Hz s}^{-1}, \qquad A_{Pio} = (8.74 \pm 1.33)\times10^{-10}\ \text{m s}^{-2}$$

Old IAU relation between the AU and the Sun's GM (Eq. 31):
$$\mu_\odot = A^3 k^2 D^{-2}$$

## Data used
- Literature review only, with no new data analysis. Sources include the EPM, INPOP and DE planetary ephemerides; the Pioneer 10/11 Doppler and telemetry re-analyses; spacecraft flyby tracking reports; Uranian satellite astrometry; and lunar laser ranging.

## Results & predictions
- Pioneer anomaly: regarded as resolved by thermal recoil (~80% modelled, remainder not significant). A gravitational origin is disfavoured by the motions of outer-planet ephemerides and TNOs.
- Flyby anomaly: "No doubts exist on the existence of such an anomaly". It "has always resisted any attempt of explanation". The Pioneer thermal models "do not seem" to work for it.
- AU increase: no longer meaningful after the 2012 redefinition.
- Juno's 2013 Earth flyby: Eq. 28 predicts ~7 mm/s. Data analysis was still ongoing at the time.

## Criticisms & responses
- This is a review, not a claim paper. It is itself a summary of criticism of anomaly claims.
- **Implications for QI.** QI's early empirical support was built on the Pioneer and flyby anomalies ([2007-mcculloch-pioneer-anomaly](../2007-mcculloch-pioneer-anomaly/README.md), [2008-mcculloch-flyby-anomalies-mnras](../2008-mcculloch-flyby-anomalies-mnras/README.md)). Iorio's assessment is that the Pioneer anomaly is thermal. See [2012-turyshev-pioneer-thermal](../../03-criticism/2012-turyshev-pioneer-thermal/README.md). He also says flyby explanations, including McCulloch's, remain unsatisfactory.
- McCulloch later cited this review himself ([2016-mcculloch-relativity-uncertainty](../2016-mcculloch-relativity-uncertainty/README.md), ref. [19]) for "anomalies seen in Solar system probes".
