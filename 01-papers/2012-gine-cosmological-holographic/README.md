**Citation:** Giné, J. (2012). *Cosmological Consequences of the Holographic Scenario*. International Journal of Theoretical Physics 52(1), 53-61. Published online 18 Aug 2012; issue dated January 2013. [doi:10.1007/s10773-012-1298-0](https://doi.org/10.1007/s10773-012-1298-0)

**TL;DR:** A paywalled paper by Jaume Giné on the cosmological consequences of Verlinde-type holographic gravity. Its reference list shows it draws heavily on modified inertia (Milgrom, Sciama, Mach's principle) and on McCulloch's MiHsC papers.
