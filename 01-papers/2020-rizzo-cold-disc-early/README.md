# A dynamically cold disk galaxy in the early Universe

Read the paper at [arXiv:2009.01251](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.01251) · [doi:10.1038/s41586-020-2572-6](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2572-6).

**Citation:** Rizzo, F., Vegetti, S., Powell, D., Fraternali, F., McKean, J. P., Stacey, H. R. & White, S. D. M. (2020). *A dynamically cold disk galaxy in the early Universe*. Nature 584, 201–204. https://doi.org/10.1038/s41586-020-2572-6. Preprint: [arXiv:2009.01251](https://arxiv.org/abs/2009.01251) (`paper.pdf` is the arXiv version).

**TL;DR:** ALMA [CII] imaging of the strongly lensed galaxy SPT0418-47 at z = 4.225 reveals a rotating disc with V/σ ≈ 10, as dynamically cold as local spirals. A rotation-curve decomposition into a Sérsic bulge, exponential gas disc and NFW halo gives the mass budget used in our [A6f](../../09-analysis/a6f-rizzo-z45-qi/README.md). The paper does not mention QI.

## Key claims
- SPT0418-47 has V/σ = 9.7 ± 0.4, far colder than predicted by galaxy-evolution models at this epoch (main text).
- The rotation curve has the shape of a local bulge-dominated spiral: a bump near 0.2 kpc, a decline, then flattening beyond 1.5 kpc (main text).

## Key equations
Circular velocity decomposed as $V_c^2 = V_{\rm star}^2 + V_{\rm gas}^2 + V_{\rm DM}^2$, with an NFW halo of fixed concentration c = 3.06 (Methods).

## Data used
- ALMA band-7 [CII] and dust continuum of SPT0418-47, modelled with 3D lens-kinematic reconstruction (Methods).

## Results & predictions
- Extended Data Table 5: M★ = 1.2 (+0.2/−0.1) × 10¹⁰ M☉, R_e = 0.22 (+0.04/−0.02) kpc, n = 2.2 (+0.3/−0.2), M_gas = 1.3 ± 0.2 × 10¹⁰ M☉, M_DM = 1.7 ± 0.3 × 10¹² M☉.
