# Strongly baryon-dominated disk galaxies at the peak of galaxy formation ten billion years ago

**Citation:** Genzel, R., Förster Schreiber, N. M., Übler, H., et al. (2017). *Strongly baryon-dominated disk galaxies at the peak of galaxy formation ten billion years ago*. Nature 543, 397–401. https://doi.org/10.1038/nature21685. Preprint: [arXiv:1703.04310](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.04310) (`paper.pdf` is arXiv v1).

**TL;DR:** Deep Hα rotation curves of six massive star-forming discs at z = 0.85–2.38 fall beyond their peak. Disc-plus-halo models give low dark-matter fractions inside the half-light radius. The authors interpret this with high gas fractions, pressure support and halo cores in ΛCDM. McCulloch 2017 used these six galaxies to support QI's redshift prediction.

## Key claims
- The six rotation curves fall beyond their peak. A stacked curve of 97 further discs is consistent with a pure baryonic disc out to ~1.8 R₁/₂ and falls below it further out (main text).
- Dark-matter fractions within R₁/₂ are 0–0.21, with four upper limits (Table 1).
- The authors attribute the falling curves to baryon dominance and pressure support from turbulent gas.

## Key equations
Circular velocity corrected for asymmetric drift (Table 1, note a): $v_c(R)^2 = v_{rot}(R)^2 + 3.36\,\sigma_0^2 (R/R_{1/2})$.

## Data used
- SINFONI and KMOS (VLT) Hα integral-field spectroscopy of six galaxies with log M★ ≈ 10.6–11.1 and R₁/₂ ≈ 4–9 kpc.
- Disc, bulge and NFW halo models fitted to the major-axis kinematics, with priors on stellar and baryonic masses (Table 1).

## Results & predictions
- Table 1 lists redshift, R₁/₂, v_c(R₁/₂), σ₀, baryonic mass and f_DM(R₁/₂) for each galaxy. Our [A6d](../../09-analysis/a6d-mcculloch-2017-highz/README.md) uses these values.

## Criticisms & responses
- Milgrom 2017 ([arXiv:1703.06110](https://arxiv.org/abs/1703.06110)) argues that these discs "all but exclude" an acceleration scale four times the local one at z ≈ 2 (see [ideas-and-tests](../../08-synthesis/ideas-and-tests.md)).
- Editorial: [A6d](../../09-analysis/a6d-mcculloch-2017-highz/README.md) finds Eq. 8 accelerations 23–49% above the tabulated values conditional on this paper's NFW-derived baryonic inputs. A no-halo QI refit to the measured kinematics remains open.
