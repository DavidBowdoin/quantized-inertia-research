# Evidence index

There is one note per phenomenon. Each note gives the observational data, the mainstream explanation, QI's prediction and the papers that make it, the independent tests, and a short assessment.

**Verdict scale:**
- `supported-by-author-only`: only QI's proponents have published support.
- `contested`: an open dispute in the literature.
- `independently-unconfirmed`: no independent test has been done, or the phenomenon itself is unconfirmed.
- `refuted-or-null`: controlled tests found nothing.
- `mainstream-explained`: the anomaly now has a conventional explanation.

## Cross-cutting observations
- **Θ is not one fixed number.** The horizon scale Θ has been 2.6–2.7, 4.3, 4.4 and 8.8 × 10²⁶ m in different papers, and photon-specific in 2022. This moves the predicted acceleration scale by a factor of about 3 and weakens the "no adjustable parameter" claim.
- **The anomaly-based support has weakened.** The early support came from the Pioneer anomaly, which is now explained by thermal recoil, and from flybys, where later flybys are null.
- **The astrophysical fits are MOND-like.** In the deep regime the QI formula is MOND with a₀ = 2c²/Θ, so it fits wherever MOND with a larger a₀ fits.
- **The direct tests are null.** QI's most falsifiable predictions, laboratory and propulsion thrust, were tested independently, partly under McCulloch's own DARPA award, and found nothing.
- **Independent work is mostly indirect.** Smolin, Cadoni & Tuveri, Hernandez, Kholupenko and Lynch work on related ideas, but they cite QI only in passing or test something else.
