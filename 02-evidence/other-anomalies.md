# Other anomalies claimed for QI

McCulloch has applied QI to many further phenomena; his 2024 book surveys 54 "anomalies" ([2024-mcculloch-quantised-accelerations](../07-media/2024-mcculloch-quantised-accelerations/README.md)). Each case in this note is treated briefly. The overall verdict is "supported by author only", because every QI treatment here is by McCulloch or his co-authors. Several of the target anomalies are themselves unconfirmed or resolved, as the per-item verdicts in the summary table show.

## Summary

## Tajmar effect
- **Data.** Tajmar, Plesescu & Seifert (2009) reported laser-gyroscope signals above cooled spinning rings. The gyro/ring acceleration ratio was about 3 ± 1.2 × 10⁻⁸ for clockwise and 1.5 ± 1.2 × 10⁻⁸ for anticlockwise rotation, appearing below ~25 K.
- **Mainstream view.** The effect "has not yet been reproduced in another laboratory" (McCulloch's own words). A Canterbury ring-laser search (Graham et al. 2008) found no signal. Tajmar et al. ([arXiv:0806.2271](https://arxiv.org/abs/0806.2271)) say that search was less sensitive.
- **QI prediction.**
  - [McCulloch 2010, EPL 89, 19001](../01-papers/2010-mcculloch-tajmar-effect-explained/README.md): da′ ≈ −(βπ²c²/Θ)(a_gs/|a_s|) ≈ −1.78 ± 0.25 × 10⁻⁸. It does not explain the parity violation.
  - [McCulloch 2011, EPL 95, 39002](../01-papers/2011-mcculloch-tajmar-effect/README.md) changes the reference frame to the ring. That gives 2.67 ± 0.24 × 10⁻⁸ clockwise and 1.34 ± 0.12 × 10⁻⁸ anticlockwise, and it withdraws the 2010 mirror-image southern-hemisphere prediction.
- **Assessment.** The fit is within large error bars, but the reference frame was changed after the fact, and the target effect is unreplicated.

## Podkletnov effect
- **Data.** Podkletnov (1992, 1997) reported 0.05–0.07% weight loss above an AC-levitated superconducting disc, and 0.3–0.5% when it spun at 5000 rpm. The effect was seen in a single laboratory.
- **Replications.** Static replications did not find it: Li et al. 1997 (NASA Marshall) and Woods et al. 2001. The paper notes that their conditions were not exact.
- **QI prediction** ([McCulloch 2011, Physics Procedia 20, 134](../01-papers/2011-mcculloch-podkletnov-effect/README.md)):
  - AC field only: 0.0029 ± 0.00025 m s⁻², which is 0.03% of g and about half the observed effect.
  - With rotation: 0.042%, 28× too small.
  - The predicted confinement of the effect to the cryostat contradicts the reported vertical column.
  - The arXiv text says the published version contains an error (a doubled vibrational acceleration).
- **Wider context.** Provatidis (2024) mentions the Podkletnov application in his review ([2024-provatidis-inertial-propulsion-review](../01-papers/2024-provatidis-inertial-propulsion-review/README.md)).
- **Assessment.** QI gives only partial agreement with an effect that has never been confirmed.

## Proton radius puzzle
- **Data.** Muonic hydrogen gave r_p = 0.84 fm, against 0.88 fm from electron methods, a 7σ discrepancy that needs an extra 320 µeV of binding energy.
- **QI prediction** ([McCulloch 2017, Progress in Physics 13, 100](../01-papers/2017-mcculloch-proton-radius/README.md)): the proton shelters the muon from Unruh radiation, covering a solid-angle fraction of 5.7 × 10⁻⁶. That gives E ≈ 2.8 × 10⁻²³ J ≈ 180 µeV, about **55%** of the anomaly. The effect in ordinary hydrogen is about 10⁵ times smaller. The calculation is order-of-magnitude and uses a classical orbit.
- **Resolution.** Later electronic-hydrogen measurements gave about 0.83–0.84 fm: Bezginov et al. 2019, *Science* 365, 1007 (Lamb shift), and Xiong et al. 2019, *Nature* 575, 147 (PRad). CODATA 2018 adopted r_p = 0.8414 fm. The puzzle is now mainly attributed to the older electron measurements, so QI's partial explanation addresses a discrepancy that is largely gone.

## Cold fusion / LENR
- **QI claim** ([McCulloch 2018, Progress in Physics 14, 63](../01-papers/2018-mcculloch-cold-fusion/README.md)):
  - Treating uncertainty-principle momentum as real gives T > ħc/3kD. Cracks narrower than **28 nm** would then reach **27,000 K**.
  - Mutual radiation sheltering would push deuterons together harder than Coulomb repulsion at 1.6 × 10⁻¹⁵ m.
  - The note admits it "does not directly explain the lack of gamma rays".
  - Its sonoluminescence estimate is 1500 K, against 2300–5100 K measured.
- **Status.** LENR is not accepted by mainstream physics and its reproducibility has been disputed since 1989. No critique of this specific note or test of its prediction was found.

## Light bending and Shapiro delay
- **QI claim** ([McCulloch 2022, Research Square preprint](../01-papers/2022-mcculloch-light-bending/README.md)): for photons, *assume* 2c²/Θ = GM/r². That doubles the Newtonian acceleration and gives c_r/c_∞ = 1 − 2GM/(rc²), which the paper says reproduces the solar deflection and the Shapiro delay.
- **Critique.**
  - The factor of 2 comes from a photon-specific, position-dependent Θ. That is not the fixed Θ ≈ 8.8 × 10²⁶ m that QI uses for galaxies, and the paper itself flags this.
  - The deflection angle (1.75″) is never computed.
  - The result is a variable-speed-of-light reading, not a geodesic calculation.
  - It makes no prediction that differs from GR, and galaxy- and cluster-scale lensing are not addressed.
- **Status.** This is a re-derivation of a known GR result under an extra assumption, not new evidence.

## Proxima Centauri
- See [wide-binaries](wide-binaries.md).
- **QI claim** ([McCulloch 2024, MNRAS Letters 532, L67](../01-papers/2024-mcculloch-proxima-centauri/README.md)): v = (2GMc²/Θ)^¼ = 0.483 ± 0.01 km s⁻¹, against 0.53 ± 0.14 km s⁻¹ observed. Newton gives 0.344 and MOND 0.424 km s⁻¹.
- **Critique.**
  - Kervella, Thévenin & Lovis 2017 (A&A 598, L7) find Proxima *bound* in Newtonian gravity, on an eccentric orbit with e ≈ 0.50 and a period of ~550 kyr. The letter instead assumes a circular orbit.
  - Banik & Kroupa 2019 propose a decisive astrometric test.

## Jupiter-mass binary objects (JuMBOs)
- **QI claim** ([McCulloch 2025, *Astronomy* 5, 1](../01-papers/2025-mcculloch-jupiter-mass-binaries/README.md)):
  - The 42 candidate pairs in the Trapezium Cluster (25–390 AU, 0.7–13 M_Jup; Pearson & McCaughrean 2023) have Newtonian mutual accelerations that "level off" near 2c²/Θ = 2 × 10⁻¹⁰ m s⁻². Two pairs fall marginally below that floor.
  - QI predicts up to about 70 m s⁻¹ of excess orbital speed at 400 AU, which Doppler spectroscopy could test.
- **Critique.**
  - The accelerations use projected separations and photometric masses with 50% assumed errors, and no velocities are measured.
  - The QI accelerations are bounded below by 2c²/Θ *by construction*.
  - The objects' nature is disputed. Luhman 2024 argues many are reddened background sources, and a 2025 NIRSpec study ([arXiv:2507.03679](https://arxiv.org/abs/2507.03679)) found seven to be background sources. Both are flagged as unverified summaries in the README.
- **Status.** A prediction that has not yet been tested, about objects that may not be binaries at all.

## EPR paradox
- **QI-adjacent claim** ([McCulloch & Giné 2021, MPL B 35, 2150072](../01-papers/2021-mcculloch-epr-paradox/README.md); abstract only): non-interacting entangled particles have small ΔE and hence large Δt, so "present and past become indistinguishable". The erased information should release Landauer heat, which might make the idea testable.
- **Context.** Loophole-free Bell tests (e.g. Hensen et al. 2015) confirm quantum correlations. It was not possible to check whether the paper addresses Bell's theorem, and no numerical prediction is available.

## Other items mentioned in the sources
- **Maximum black-hole mass** of about 10⁵² kg, and a sub-Stefan–Boltzmann radiation deficit of 2.7 × 10⁻²⁰ at 100 pK ([2007-mcculloch-pioneer-anomaly](../01-papers/2007-mcculloch-pioneer-anomaly/README.md)). Neither has been tested.
- **Other Solar System anomalies** reviewed by Iorio (perihelia, the orbit of Mab, lunar eccentricity, the AU increase). QI is not applied to these in the sources, and the AU "anomaly" disappeared with the 2012 redefinition ([2015-iorio-solar-system-anomalies](../01-papers/2015-iorio-solar-system-anomalies/README.md)).
- **Giné (2012)**: the cited Solar-System anomaly literature cannot be checked, because the text is inaccessible ([2012-gine-cosmological-holographic](../01-papers/2012-gine-cosmological-holographic/README.md)).

## Assessment
Across these cases a pattern recurs. QI is applied, often in short notes in low-impact venues (Progress in Physics, preprints), to anomalies that are unreplicated (Tajmar, Podkletnov), resolved (proton radius), outside the mainstream (LENR) or of uncertain nature (JuMBOs). Agreement is usually partial: 55%, half, 28× too small, or matched only after a change of frame or a photon-specific choice of Θ. None of these applications has been independently tested or reproduced. They show how flexible the theory is rather than adding independent support for it.
