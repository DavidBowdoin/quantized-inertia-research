# University of Plymouth

Public university in Plymouth, Devon, England. Origins in the Plymouth School of Navigation (1862); became Plymouth Polytechnic (1970), Polytechnic South West (1989), and gained university status in **1992**. Faculties: Arts, Humanities and Business; Health; Science and Engineering. It has a strong marine and maritime profile (e.g. marine science and engineering, Marine Station; marine biologist Richard Thompson is credited with coining "microplastics").

## Role in QI

- Host institution of [Mike McCulloch](mike-mcculloch.md) as Lecturer in Geomatics (2008–2023, per his bio; in the marine science and engineering school), now listed as "Visiting Specialist" in the School of Engineering, Computing and Mathematics.
- Administered and publicised the [2018 DARPA award](darpa-2018-award.md) (US$1.3M, four years).
- Issued a 2022 press release linking McCulloch's QI research to [IVO Ltd](ivo-ltd.md)'s Quantum Drive ("University research inspires new commercial advances in satellite propulsion"). The release reports company claims (vacuum-chamber "validation" of QI thrust) without independent verification.
- McCulloch's blog mentions test set-ups built at "Plymouth University" with Richard Arundal.
- Wikipedia lists McCulloch among notable academics as developer of quantised inertia.

QI is not a departmental research programme of the university; it is essentially the work of one staff member and external collaborators. University press releases describe the theory favourably and should be read as institutional communications, not as endorsements by independent physicists.
