# Jaume Giné

> [!NOTE]
> **Name check.** The QI theorist is **Jaume Giné** (full name Jaume Giné Mesa; "Jaume" is the Catalan form of James) of the **Universitat de Lleida**. This file is named `jaume-gine.md` at the project's request, but no "José Giné" was found among QI authors. He should not be confused with **José Luis Pérez-Díaz** of the **Universidad de Alcalá**, the Spanish *experimental* partner in McCulloch's DARPA project (see below). Both appear in QI literature and are easily conflated.

## Profile

- Full professor (Catedràtic d'Universitat), Departament de Matemàtica, Escola Politècnica Superior, Universitat de Lleida (Catalonia, Spain); area: applied mathematics.
- PhD, Universitat Politècnica de Catalunya (1996), on integrability of planar polynomial differential systems (advisor Javier Chavarriga), per the Mathematics Genealogy Project.
- Coordinates the Dynamical Systems (Sistemes Dinàmics) group at Lleida. Most of his output is in the qualitative theory of planar differential systems (integrability, centre problem); the inertia papers below are a separate line of work.

## QI-related work

Giné is McCulloch's most frequent co-author and the main independent theorist developing horizon/uncertainty-principle versions of QI:

- **Before co-authoring:** *On the origin of the inertia: the modified Newtonian dynamics theory* (*Chaos, Solitons & Fractals*, 2009); holographic/Verlinde links to MiHsC: [2011 Pioneer](../01-papers/2011-gine-pioneer-holographic/README.md), [2012 MPLA](../01-papers/2012-gine-holographic-modified-inertia/README.md), [2012 cosmology](../01-papers/2012-gine-cosmological-holographic/README.md), [2017 particle masses](../01-papers/2017-gine-inertial-mass-holographic/README.md).
- **With McCulloch:** [Inertial mass from Unruh temperatures (2016)](../01-papers/2016-gine-unruh-temperatures/README.md) (removes a factor-of-two problem); [Modified inertial mass from information loss (2017)](../01-papers/2017-mcculloch-information-loss/README.md); [QI from horizon widths (2020)](../01-papers/2020-mcculloch-horizon-widths/README.md); [EPR paradox (2021)](../01-papers/2021-mcculloch-epr-paradox/README.md).
- **Heuristic uncertainty-principle derivations:** [Casimir force (2018)](../01-papers/2018-gine-casimir-uncertainty/README.md), [Hawking and Unruh temperatures (2018)](../01-papers/2018-gine-hawking-unruh-uncertainty/README.md).
- **With G. G. Luciano:** [Modified inertia from extended uncertainty principles and MOND (2020, *EPJC*)](../01-papers/2020-gine-luciano-eup-modified-inertia/README.md); [inertia via quantum fluctuations (2021)](../01-papers/2021-gine-luciano-inertia-fluctuations/README.md).

## José Luis Pérez-Díaz (Universidad de Alcalá)

- Named by Vice/Motherboard (2018) as leader of the Spanish team in the DARPA project; he had spent six months working with McCulloch in the UK; the planned set-up was a ~2 W laser in a fibre-optic loop with expected thrust ~1 mN.
- McCulloch's blog (July 2020) calls the Spanish team "the first QI experimenters", reports a thrust of 1–4 μN (~0.001 N/kW), and says the result "is inconclusive so far" and that "the pendulum is subject to significant artefacts". In November 2021 he described the Spanish results as "positive, but with qualifications", adding he was "a bit sceptical".

> [!UNVERIFIED]
> No peer-reviewed publication of the Alcalá DARPA results was found while writing this note. Pérez-Díaz's departmental affiliation within Alcalá was not checked.
